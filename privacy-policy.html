Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Stephen Akintayo Foundation and SACI Holdings USA ("we", "us", or "our") handle personal information when you visit the Global Real Estate Business Masterclass (GRBM) website, request a callback, or enroll. The entity responsible for your information may depend on the service you use and your location. Contact us with questions or requests using the details below.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Registration and enquiry details: first and last name, email address, phone number, occupation or role, referral source, and the contents of messages you send us.
  • Enrollment and transaction details: course selected, payment status, transaction reference, billing country, and information needed to reconcile a payment. Paystack or Stripe processes payment card details through its checkout. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code.
  • Website and device data: pages visited, browser and device information, IP address, approximate location, and interactions. The Meta Pixel may collect or receive activity data for advertising measurement. Browser settings may offer controls for cookies and similar technologies.

2. How and why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, identify and match a participant to their registration and payment, verify registration details, prevent duplicate or fraudulent registrations, administer enrollment, deliver the programme, send relevant service updates, provide support, improve the website, measure advertising, and meet legal, accounting, and security obligations. Depending on the activity and applicable law, we process information to perform our agreement with you, with your consent, to meet legal obligations, or for legitimate operational interests. We use contact information for programme related communications; you can opt out of non-essential marketing messages.

3. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share only what is reasonably needed with service providers supporting the website and programme, including Formspree for callback form submissions, Paystack and Stripe for payment processing, and Meta for advertising measurement. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect people or our rights, or as part of a business transfer. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel and may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website activity and measure advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser. Blocking them may limit some website functions. For choices about data Meta receives, review Meta's privacy and advertising settings.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including programme delivery, support, accounting, and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. International processing

GRBM serves participants in multiple countries. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. Where applicable, we take steps required by law for such transfers.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection regulator. Some records must be retained for legal or transaction purposes. You may opt out of marketing at any time. Contact us to make a request.

8. Children

This website and programme are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 18. Please do not submit a child's personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the date above. The version in effect when we process your information applies, subject to any notice or consent required by law.
